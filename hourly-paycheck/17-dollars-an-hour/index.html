$17 an hour is how much a year, a month and a week?

The formula is hourly rate × hours per week × 52 weeks. For a full-time schedule of 40 hours, that is $17 × 40 × 52 = $35,360 a year in gross pay, which is 2,080 paid hours. "Gross" means before federal tax, Social Security, Medicare, state tax and any deductions.

$17 an hour after taxes

At $35,360 a year, a single filer's taxable income falls in the 10% and 12% federal brackets after the $16,100 standard deduction for 2026. On top of income tax, Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%) take 7.65% of every paycheck. That works out to a federal income tax of about $2,063 and FICA of $2,705, a combined effective rate of roughly 13.5%.

$17 an hour take-home pay by state

Your state is the biggest reason two people on $17 an hour take home different amounts.

  • No state income tax on wages (for example Texas, Florida, Nevada, Tennessee, Washington, Wyoming and South Dakota): take-home pay is close to the figures in the table above.
  • States with an income tax (for example California, New York, Virginia, Illinois and North Carolina): take-home pay is lower. At this income the state tax is usually a small percentage of pay, and some states add payroll charges such as disability insurance.

$17 an hour with overtime

Under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act, covered non-exempt employees must be paid at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a week. At $17 an hour, overtime pays $25.50 an hour. Some states have additional daily overtime rules, so check your state.

Is $17 an hour a good wage?

$17 an hour is more than twice the $7.25 federal minimum wage, which has not changed since 2009. Whether it is enough depends on where you live. Some states already have minimum wages near $17, including California at $16.90 and Washington at $17.13 in 2026, so in those states $17 is close to entry level. In lower-cost areas the same wage covers considerably more.

A useful rule of thumb is to compare your take-home pay with your rent. At about $2,549 a month before state tax, a common guideline of keeping housing near 30% of take-home pay points to a rent budget of roughly $765. In many cities that means roommates, a shorter commute, or extra hours.

Every $1 of raise adds $2,080 a year at full-time hours, so moving from $17 to $18 an hour is worth $2,080 before tax.

$17 an hour: frequently asked questions

How much is $17 an hour per year?

Working 40 hours a week for 52 weeks (2,080 hours), $17 an hour is $35,360 a year before taxes and deductions.

How much is $17 an hour after taxes per year?

A single filer earning $17 an hour full-time keeps roughly $30,592 a year after federal income tax and FICA in a state with no income tax. That is about $14.71 per hour. State income tax, if your state has one, comes off this figure.

How much is $17 an hour biweekly?

Gross biweekly pay at 40 hours a week is $1,360. After federal tax and FICA, a single filer takes home about $1,176.61 per biweekly paycheck before any state tax.

How much is $17 an hour per month after taxes?

Gross monthly pay is about $2,946.67. After federal income tax and FICA, a single filer keeps about $2,549.31 a month before state tax.

How much is $17 an hour per week?

At 40 hours a week, $17 an hour is $680 gross per week, or about $588.30 after federal tax and FICA for a single filer.

How much is $17 an hour part-time?

At 20 hours a week it is $17,680 a year, at 25 hours $22,100, at 30 hours $26,520 and at 35 hours $30,940, all before taxes.

How much is $17 an hour with overtime?

With 5 hours of overtime a week at time-and-a-half ($25.50 an hour), gross pay is $807.50 a week, or $41,990 a year. With 10 overtime hours it is $935 a week, or $48,620 a year.

Is $17 an hour a good wage?

It is more than double the $7.25 federal minimum wage, but whether it is enough depends on where you live and your expenses. $17 an hour is close to the 2026 statewide minimum wage in California ($16.90) and Washington ($17.13), so it goes much further in a lower-cost area than in a high-cost city.

What is $17 an hour as a yearly salary?

$17 an hour is equal to a $35,360 annual salary at 2,080 hours a year.

How much is $17 an hour a day?

An 8-hour day at $17 an hour is $136 before taxes.

How we calculated these numbers

Gross pay is hourly rate × weekly hours × 52 weeks. Overtime hours are paid at 1.5 times the rate. Take-home estimates in the tables subtract federal income tax (2026 brackets and standard deduction), Social Security at 6.2% and Medicare at 1.45%. They assume a single filer unless stated, no dependents or credits, no pre-tax deductions, and no state or local taxes. The calculator above also lets you add state, dependents and filing status.
