Reporting a Calculator Issue

If you believe a calculator is producing an unexpected result, please include the relevant calculator settings when contacting us.

For example, you may include the pay frequency, filing status, state, and general income information used for the calculation.

Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Tax Questions

PaycheckMint provides estimated paycheck calculations for informational and planning purposes.

We cannot provide personalized tax, legal, accounting, employment, or financial advice.

For questions about your specific tax situation, contact the IRS, your state tax authority, your employer's payroll department, or a qualified tax professional.

Please Don't Send Sensitive Information

For your security, please do not include sensitive information in an email.

  • Social Security numbers
  • Bank account numbers
  • Credit or debit card information
  • Passwords
  • Government identification numbers
  • Other confidential financial information

Response Time
