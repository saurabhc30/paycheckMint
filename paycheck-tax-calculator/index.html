Understanding Paycheck Tax Calculations

Calculating your take-home pay requires subtracting mandatory federal, state, and local taxes, as well as optional benefit deductions, from your total gross earnings.

Net Pay = Gross Pay - Federal Tax - FICA Taxes - State/Local Tax - Deductions

FICA Taxes Breakdown

FICA (Federal Insurance Contributions Act) taxes consist of two fixed components assessed on earnings:

  • Social Security: 6.2% on wages up to the annual limit.
  • Medicare: 1.45% on all gross earnings (with an additional 0.9% for high earners).

Frequently Asked Questions

How are pre-tax deductions handled?

Pre-tax deductions like traditional 401(k)s, HSAs, and health insurance reduce your taxable wage base before federal and state taxes are calculated.

What is the difference between marginal and effective tax rates?

Your marginal rate is the tax rate applied to your last dollar of income, while your effective tax rate is the total tax paid divided by total gross pay.
