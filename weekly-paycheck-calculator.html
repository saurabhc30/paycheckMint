What is a weekly paycheck?

A weekly paycheck is a payment an employee receives once each week. A weekly pay schedule generally results in 52 paychecks during a full year, although the exact number can vary depending on the employer's payroll calendar.

Your weekly gross pay is the amount you earn before taxes and deductions. Your weekly take-home pay is the amount remaining after applicable taxes, payroll deductions, and other withholding.

How to calculate weekly take-home pay

To estimate weekly take-home pay, start with your annual gross income and divide it by 52. Federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, state tax, and eligible deductions are then estimated to determine your weekly net pay.

Annual gross pay ÷ 52 = estimated weekly gross pay

Weekly gross pay − taxes − deductions = estimated weekly take-home pay

How much is $1,000 a week after taxes?

If your gross weekly pay is $1,000, your actual take-home pay depends on your federal tax withholding, filing status, state, Social Security and Medicare taxes, and deductions such as retirement contributions or health insurance.

There is no single after-tax amount for everyone earning $1,000 per week. Use the calculator above to enter your circumstances and estimate your weekly take-home pay.

Weekly paycheck taxes

A typical weekly paycheck can include several types of taxes. Federal income tax depends on taxable income and filing circumstances. Social Security and Medicare are generally payroll taxes withheld from employee wages. State income tax depends on the state and applicable rules.

  • Federal income tax
  • Social Security tax
  • Medicare tax
  • State income tax where applicable
  • Pre-tax and post-tax deductions

Actual payroll withholding can differ from a simple annual tax estimate because employers use payroll withholding rules, employee Form W-4 information, pay frequency, year-to-date wages, and other factors.

Weekly paycheck examples

$52,000 annual salary

An annual salary of $52,000 equals approximately $1,000 in gross weekly pay when divided across 52 weeks. Your actual weekly take-home pay will be lower after taxes and deductions.

$75,000 annual salary

A $75,000 annual salary produces approximately $1,442.31 in gross weekly pay before taxes and deductions.

$100,000 annual salary

A $100,000 annual salary produces approximately $1,923.08 in gross weekly pay before taxes and deductions.

Weekly vs. biweekly pay

Weekly employees generally receive 52 paychecks per year. Biweekly employees generally receive 26 paychecks per year. The annual salary is normally the same, but the amount and timing of each paycheck are different.

Weekly pay can make budgeting easier because money arrives more frequently. However, monthly and annual totals should be compared when evaluating overall compensation.

Weekly Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What is a weekly paycheck calculator?

It estimates your weekly gross and take-home pay after estimated federal, payroll, state taxes, and deductions.

How many weekly paychecks are there in a year?

A typical weekly payroll schedule has 52 paychecks in a year. Some payroll calendars can result in an additional paycheck during a particular calendar year.

How do I calculate my weekly salary?

For a simple annual salary calculation, divide your annual gross salary by 52 to estimate weekly gross pay.

Does weekly pay get taxed differently?

Your annual tax liability is not simply determined by how frequently you are paid. Payroll withholding can vary with pay frequency and withholding information, while Social Security and Medicare generally apply to covered wages.

What is the difference between weekly gross pay and take-home pay?

Gross pay is your earnings before taxes and deductions. Take-home pay is the amount remaining after applicable taxes and deductions.
