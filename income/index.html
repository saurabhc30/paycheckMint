How Income Turns Into Take-Home Pay

A simple way to understand your paycheck is to follow the money from earnings to the amount you actually receive.

Stage What it tells you
Gross pay Your earnings before payroll taxes and many deductions.
Payroll taxes Taxes withheld from eligible wages, such as Social Security and Medicare taxes.
Income-tax withholding Amount withheld toward applicable income taxes.
Other deductions Benefits, retirement contributions and other paycheck deductions.
Net pay The amount remaining from the paycheck after applicable withholding and deductions.
