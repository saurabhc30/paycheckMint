How the weekly paycheck calculator works

A weekly paycheck is paid once every seven days, resulting in 52 regular pay periods during a typical year. The IRS payroll tables specifically include weekly payroll as a 52-period schedule.

This calculator starts with your gross pay and estimates federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, state and local taxes, and the deductions you enter. The goal is to give you a useful estimate of what may reach your bank account from a normal weekly paycheck.

Weekly gross pay = Annual gross pay ÷ 52

Salary vs. hourly weekly pay

Pay type Basic calculation Example
Salary Annual salary ÷ 52 $78,000 ÷ 52 = $1,500
Hourly Hourly rate × hours worked per week $25 × 40 hours = $1,000

What taxes can reduce your paycheck?

Federal income tax withholding depends on your wages and the information supplied to your employer on Form W-4. Withholding amounts vary based on filing status, dependents, and extra adjustments.

Social Security and Medicare are separate payroll taxes known as FICA taxes. For employees, Social Security is taxed at 6.2% and Medicare at 1.45% of gross income.

2026 federal tax assumptions

For 2026, the IRS lists a standard deduction of $16,100 for single filers, $24,150 for heads of household, and $32,200 for married couples filing jointly. The federal income-tax system uses seven marginal rates ranging from 10% to 37%.

This calculator annualizes your estimated taxable wages and applies those federal brackets to create a paycheck-level estimate. Actual payroll withholding can differ because employer payroll systems use IRS withholding procedures and your actual Form W-4 information.

Why your weekly paycheck may change

Your paycheck can change even when your salary or hourly rate stays the same. Common causes include changes to your benefits, retirement contributions, tax withholding, overtime, bonuses, or other payroll deductions.

What happens in a 53-paycheck year?

Because a non-leap year has 365 days (52 weeks plus 1 extra day), calendar dates shift over time. Depending on the weekday your payday falls on, certain calendar years may result in 53 weekly paychecks instead of 52.

Common mistakes when estimating weekly take-home pay

  • Dividing annual salary by 48 instead of 52.
  • Forgetting pre-tax retirement or healthcare deductions.
  • Ignoring state or local income tax rates.
  • Confusing weekly gross pay with monthly gross pay divided by 4.

Frequently asked questions

How many weekly paychecks are there in a year?

A standard weekly payroll schedule produces 52 paychecks per year.

How do I calculate weekly pay from salary?

Divide your annual gross salary by 52. For example, a $78,000 salary produces $1,500 in gross pay per regular weekly paycheck before taxes and deductions.

Is weekly pay better than biweekly pay?

Weekly pay provides a steady cash flow every seven days, which can make budgeting easier for daily or weekly living expenses, though your overall annual take-home pay remains equivalent for the same annual income.

Why does my actual paycheck differ from the calculator?

Payroll withholding depends on your actual Form W-4 information, benefits, deductions, payroll system, state and local rules, and other circumstances.

Can I use this calculator for hourly workers?

Yes. Switch to hourly pay and enter your hourly rate, normal weekly hours, and overtime hours if applicable.
