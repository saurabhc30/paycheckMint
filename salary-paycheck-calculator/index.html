How to calculate paycheck take-home pay from salary

A salary paycheck represents your annual agreed compensation broken down across regular payment cycles. Employers typically run salary payroll on weekly (52 paychecks), biweekly (26 paychecks), semimonthly (24 paychecks), or monthly (12 paychecks) schedules.

This calculator converts your total gross salary into per-paycheck gross wages and applies 2026 federal income tax brackets, FICA taxes (Social Security and Medicare), estimated state and local taxes, and pre/post-tax deductions to calculate your net salary take-home pay.

Paycheck Gross Salary = Annual Gross Salary ÷ Pay Periods

Comparing Salary Pay Schedule Frequencies

Pay Schedule Checks per Year Gross Salary Example ($78,000)
Biweekly 26 $78,000 ÷ 26 = $3,000 / check
Semimonthly 24 $78,000 ÷ 24 = $3,250 / check
Monthly 12 $78,000 ÷ 12 = $6,500 / check
Weekly 52 $78,000 ÷ 52 = $1,500 / check

Understanding Salary Payroll Taxes

Federal income tax is withheld based on your gross income, tax filing status (Single, Married Filing Jointly, or Head of Household), standard deductions, and dependents on Form W-4.

FICA payroll taxes are mandatory and consist of two parts:

  • Social Security Tax: 6.2% on eligible gross wages.
  • Medicare Tax: 1.45% on all eligible gross wages.

2026 Federal Income Tax Brackets & Standard Deductions

For 2026, the standard deduction amounts are $16,100 for single filers, $24,150 for heads of household, and $32,200 for married couples filing jointly. The calculator annualizes your taxable wages after pre-tax benefits to determine federal withholding using marginal tax rates from 10% to 37%.

How Pre-Tax Deductions Save Tax Dollars

Contributions to traditional 401(k) or 403(b) retirement plans, Health Savings Accounts (HSA), Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA), and qualified health insurance premiums are subtracted from gross earnings before tax calculation. This lowers your total taxable salary wage base.

Frequently asked questions

How is salary calculated per paycheck?

Divide your total gross annual salary by the number of pay periods in your employer's pay schedule (26 for biweekly, 24 for semimonthly, 12 for monthly, or 52 for weekly).

What is the difference between biweekly and semimonthly salary pay?

Biweekly employees receive 26 paychecks per year (every two weeks), resulting in two months with 3 paychecks. Semimonthly employees receive 24 paychecks per year (typically paid on the 1st and 15th of each month).

How do 401(k) contributions affect salary paycheck tax?

Traditional 401(k) contributions are pre-tax deductions that reduce your income tax wage base, lowering the federal and state tax withheld from each paycheck. Roth 401(k) contributions are post-tax and do not reduce current income tax.

Why does my calculated paycheck differ slightly from my actual paystub?

Small variations can occur due to precise Form W-4 allowance choices, local tax jurisdictions, supplemental tax rates, or employer benefit structures.
