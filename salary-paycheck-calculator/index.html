HOW IT WORKS

How the salary paycheck calculator works

SALARY EXAMPLE

What does a $75,000 salary look like after taxes?

A $75,000 annual salary does not mean you receive $75,000 in cash. Your actual take-home pay is lower because federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, state taxes, and any employee deductions may be withheld from your pay.

$75,000 annual salary

If you are paid biweekly, your gross paycheck is approximately $2,884.62 before taxes and deductions. Your actual take-home amount depends on your state, filing status, benefits, and withholding settings.

GROSS VS. TAKE-HOME

Salary vs. take-home pay

Gross salary is the amount you earn before deductions. Take-home pay, sometimes called net pay, is what remains after applicable taxes and deductions are taken out.

For example, someone earning $80,000 per year may have a gross annual salary of $80,000 while receiving a substantially smaller amount in actual paychecks after taxes and benefits.

2026 TAX ESTIMATE

What taxes affect your salary paycheck?

Federal income tax

Federal income tax is progressive, meaning different portions of taxable income are taxed at different rates. Your filing status, deductions, credits, and withholding settings can affect the amount withheld.

Social Security

Employee Social Security tax is generally 6.2% of covered wages up to the annual Social Security wage base.

Medicare

Employee Medicare tax is generally 1.45% of covered wages. Higher-income employees may also be subject to the Additional Medicare Tax.

State income tax

State income tax varies significantly. Some states have no individual income tax, while others impose state income tax. This calculator uses simplified state estimates rather than reproducing every state and local payroll rule.

METHODOLOGY

How PaycheckMint estimates salary take-home pay

PaycheckMint annualizes your salary and estimates federal income tax, FICA, state tax, and deductions. The resulting annual net amount is then divided by your selected number of pay periods.

Pre-tax deductions may reduce taxable income, while post-tax deductions are subtracted after estimated taxes. The calculator also lets you enter additional withholding amounts for more customized estimates.

Important: This is an educational estimate, not an official payroll calculation. Actual withholding can differ because of Form W-4 elections, employer payroll systems, local taxes, benefits, year-to-date wages, tax credits, and state-specific rules.

FAQ

Salary paycheck calculator questions

What is a salary paycheck calculator?

A salary paycheck calculator estimates your take-home pay after estimated federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, state taxes, and deductions.

How do I calculate my take-home salary?

Start with your gross salary, then subtract estimated taxes and applicable deductions. PaycheckMint performs these calculations for you based on the information you enter.

Can I calculate biweekly salary?

Yes. Select Biweekly from the pay frequency menu. A typical biweekly schedule has 26 pay periods per year.

Does this calculator calculate exact withholding?

No. The result is an estimate. Actual payroll withholding depends on your Form W-4, employer payroll system, state and local rules, deductions, benefits, and other individual circumstances.
