BIWEEKLY PAY

What is a biweekly paycheck?

A biweekly paycheck is paid every two weeks. Most employees on a biweekly schedule receive 26 paychecks during a calendar year.

26 paychecks per year

Because there are 52 weeks in a typical year and employees are paid every two weeks, annual income is normally divided across 26 pay periods.

Two three-paycheck months

Employees paid biweekly usually receive three paychecks in two months of the year. The exact months depend on the employer's payroll calendar.

Gross vs. take-home pay

Gross biweekly pay is your earnings before taxes and deductions. Take-home pay is what remains after estimated payroll taxes and deductions.

Taxes are estimated

Your actual paycheck can differ because withholding depends on your Form W-4, benefits, state rules, local taxes, and other payroll details.

HOW IT WORKS

How to calculate biweekly take-home pay

A simple starting point is to divide annual gross pay by 26. Taxes and deductions are then applied to estimate your actual biweekly paycheck.

1. Start with annual income

Enter your annual gross salary before taxes, retirement contributions, insurance, or other payroll deductions.

2. Divide by 26

For a standard biweekly schedule, annual gross pay is divided by 26 to estimate gross pay for each paycheck.

3. Estimate payroll taxes

Federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, and applicable state taxes are estimated using the calculator's 2026 tax rules.

4. Subtract deductions

Pre-tax and post-tax deductions can change your final take-home amount.

PAY SCHEDULES

Biweekly vs. semimonthly pay

The two schedules are different even though both commonly result in two paychecks during most months.

Biweekly

Paid every two weeks. A standard schedule has 26 paychecks per year and two months with three paychecks.

Semimonthly

Paid twice each month. This results in 24 paychecks per year, usually on fixed calendar dates such as the 15th and last day of the month.

FAQ

Biweekly paycheck calculator FAQ

How many biweekly paychecks are there in a year?

A standard biweekly schedule has 26 paychecks per year because employees are paid every two weeks.

How is a biweekly paycheck calculated?

Annual gross income is divided by 26 to estimate gross biweekly pay. Estimated federal tax, Social Security, Medicare, state tax, and deductions are then subtracted.

How much is $75,000 a year paid biweekly?

A $75,000 annual salary equals approximately $2,884.62 in gross pay per biweekly paycheck before taxes and deductions.

Are biweekly and semimonthly pay the same?

No. Biweekly employees generally receive 26 paychecks per year, while semimonthly employees receive 24.

Why do some months have three biweekly paychecks?

There are 26 biweekly pay periods in a year. Since most months contain two paychecks, two months will normally contain a third paycheck depending on the employer's payroll calendar.
