How the Idaho paycheck calculator works

PaycheckMint starts with your gross earnings and computes federal, FICA, and Idaho deductions to provide an accurate estimate.

1. Gross pay

Your salary or hourly earnings are annualized and converted into period totals.

2. Federal tax

Estimates federal income tax withholding using 2026 IRS guidance and Form W-4 details.

3. FICA

Social Security and Medicare are calculated according to wage thresholds and rates.

4. Idaho tax

Applies state withholding formulas for Idaho. Local tax variations may apply.

What can affect your Idaho paycheck?

Federal income tax: Based on annual earnings, filing status, and W-4 parameters.

Social Security: Applies to covered wages up to the maximum annual wage base limit.

Medicare: Applies to all covered earnings without a cap, plus Additional Medicare where relevant.

Idaho taxes: Configured based on specific Idaho bracket rules and allowances.

Idaho paycheck calculator questions

How is Idaho take-home pay calculated?

Gross earnings are computed first, followed by deductions for federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, Idaho state tax, and custom deductions.

Is this Idaho calculator exact?

The results are estimates for educational planning. Specific employer setup, benefit plans, and local tax districts may cause minor differences.

Does this include federal tax calculations?

Yes, it calculates federal income tax, FICA taxes, and state withholding together.
