HOW IT WORKS

How the Georgia paycheck calculator works

PaycheckMint starts with your gross wages and estimates the major taxes and deductions that can reduce your Georgia take-home pay.

1. Gross pay

Your salary or hourly earnings are converted into an annual amount and then into your selected pay period.

2. Federal tax

The calculator estimates federal withholding using 2026 federal withholding concepts and your W-4 inputs.

3. FICA

Social Security and Medicare taxes are calculated separately because they follow different rules.

4. Georgia tax

The calculator estimates applicable Georgia state income tax. Actual withholding may differ because of state, local and employer-specific rules.

Georgia PAYCHECK INFORMATION

What can affect your Georgia paycheck?

Federal income tax Federal withholding depends on your income, filing status and W-4 information.
Social Security Employee Social Security tax generally applies to covered wages up to the annual wage base.
Medicare Employee Medicare tax generally applies to covered wages without the Social Security wage cap.
Georgia taxes State withholding can depend on your income, filing status and applicable Georgia rules.

METHODOLOGY

Gross pay vs. take-home pay in Georgia

Gross pay is the amount you earn before taxes and deductions. Take-home pay is the amount remaining after estimated taxes and deductions.

Your Georgia paycheck may be lower than your gross earnings because federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, state taxes and deductions can reduce each paycheck.

PaycheckMint is designed to provide a planning estimate before you receive your actual payroll statement.

FAQ

Georgia paycheck calculator questions

How is Georgia take-home pay calculated?

PaycheckMint starts with gross wages and estimates federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, applicable Georgia state tax and the deductions you enter. The remaining amount is your estimated take-home pay.

Is the Georgia paycheck calculator exact?

No. PaycheckMint provides an estimate for planning purposes. Actual payroll withholding can differ because employers use detailed federal and state withholding methods, W-4 information, local taxes, benefits and year-to-date wages.

Does this calculator include federal taxes?

Yes. The calculator estimates federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare along with applicable Georgia state tax.

Can I calculate hourly pay in Georgia?

Yes. Select Hourly and enter your hourly rate, regular hours and overtime hours. Overtime is estimated at 1.5 times the regular hourly rate.

Why can my actual Georgia paycheck be different?

Actual paychecks can differ because of employer payroll systems, W-4 elections, state withholding rules, local taxes, benefits, deductions and year-to-date wages.
