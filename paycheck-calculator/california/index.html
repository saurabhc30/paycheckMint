Quick Summary (BLUF): Your net take-home pay in California is calculated by subtracting federal income tax, FICA (Social Security at 6.2% and Medicare at 1.45%), California State Disability Insurance (SDI at 1.3% with no wage ceiling), and progressive state income taxes (1% to 12.3%) from your gross earnings.

How California Paycheck Withholding Works in 2026

California features comprehensive state mandatory withholdings. Knowing how gross wages convert to net pay requires breaking down four core deduction tiers.

1. California State Disability Insurance (SDI) - 2026 Rules

Under California EDD regulations, the employee SDI contribution rate is 1.3%. California has removed the taxable wage ceiling, meaning employee SDI applies to all covered wages with no cap.

2. California State Income Tax Brackets

California utilizes a progressive state income tax structure with nine main brackets ranging from 1% to 12.3%, plus an extra 1% Mental Health Services tax surcharge on taxable incomes over $1 million.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much take-home pay can I expect on a $75,000 CA salary?

For a single filer earning $75,000 in California, combined federal, FICA, and state tax deductions typically take around 22% to 26%, leaving an estimated biweekly net take-home pay between $2,100 and $2,250.

Is California SDI capped for high earners?

No. California eliminated the taxable wage ceiling for SDI, meaning employee contributions apply to every dollar earned throughout the year.
